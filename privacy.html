This Privacy Policy describes how the "WoT Replay Lab Agent" application (the "App") handles information. The App is a private desktop tool operated by the owner of the WoT Replay Lab YouTube channel. It is used solely to manage that channel.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set thumbnails, manage playlists and add title/description translations on the WoT Replay Lab channel. By using the App, the account owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the App accesses

  • Google account authorization: With the channel owner's consent, the App receives OAuth access and refresh tokens for the YouTube scopes it needs. These tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and are never shared with third parties.
  • Channel and video data: The App reads and writes data belonging to the WoT Replay Lab channel (for example, video titles, descriptions, playlists and basic video statistics) in order to publish and manage content.
  • Public game data: To create player stats cards, the App uses publicly available World of Tanks player statistics (such as nickname, clan tag, battles and win rate) obtained from Wargaming's public API, and replay files that players have voluntarily published on public replay websites.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to produce, upload and manage videos on the WoT Replay Lab channel and to analyze the channel's own performance. The App does not sell, rent or share data with advertisers or other third parties, and it does not access any YouTube account other than the channel owner's.

4. Storage and security

All data handled by the App is stored locally on the channel owner's computer. OAuth tokens are kept in a local file that is not published or transmitted anywhere other than to Google's own servers when calling the API.

5. Retention and deletion

Stored tokens and locally cached API data are kept only as long as needed to operate the channel and are deleted when no longer required. Authorized access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page, after which the App can no longer access the account.

6. Players featured in videos

Players whose replays or public statistics appear in a video may request removal of that video by emailing wotreplaylab@gmail.com with the video link.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: wotreplaylab@gmail.com
