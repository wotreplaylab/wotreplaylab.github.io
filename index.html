WoT Replay Lab is a YouTube channel that publishes standout World of Tanks battles every day. Each video includes the player's public stats card (WN8, win rate, battles on the tank), notes on key moments and a short breakdown of what made the battle special. All replays are credited to their players.

WoT Replay Lab Agent

"WoT Replay Lab Agent" is a private desktop tool used only by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on the WoT Replay Lab YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Replay removal

If a video features your replay and you would like it removed, email wotreplaylab@gmail.com with the video link. We will remove it promptly.
